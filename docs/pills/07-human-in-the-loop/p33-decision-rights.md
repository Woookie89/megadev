**Principle.** It is known up front what the human decides. For those decisions the agent
proposes, with a recommendation, and waits.

**Case.** In MEGA the human decided on accounts (AgentMail, Netlify, Render), the public
repo, keys and spending, the choice of host, how to close tickets (S01E03) and the plan
split (S01E04). The S01E04 `HANDOVER.md` has a section "Recommendations (harness's
proposal; the human decides)". MEGA Assessment: T18 (decision rights) 11 of 11.
