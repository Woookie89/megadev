### schema first

Give your tools a home: with your harness, build and deploy a remote MCP server of your own — memo, upload and transcribe — and connect it to your own harness. Then hand it to the course. A stranger will call it.

**You do**\
When your harness gives you a connect link, you open it and let the course in to your server.

**Your harness does**\
It builds a remote MCP server of your own, with the tools the course names, and hands it to the course.

**You need**\
A public https address for your server, and any account or speech-to-text key it needs. A paid key is billed for every call, and each check calls your tools, at most ten checks a day. Your harness asks you before each step outside your machine. Anyone can open the file link your upload tool gives. Once the Challenge is solved, you can delete that file and the course notes, and take back the access you gave the course.

**Done when**\
The course calls your server as a stranger and every check passes.

*Your harness is your AI agent plus the tools, sign-ins and skills you give it.*
