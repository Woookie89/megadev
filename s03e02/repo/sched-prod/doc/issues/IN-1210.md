## Question
Support says EU accounts want the dates in the CSV export as dd/mm instead of mm/dd (IN-1210). A client's payroll import reads them as mm/dd, as they come today, and asked us not to flip them (IN-4203). Do we change the format of the existing date columns?

## Options
1. Switch the existing date columns to dd/mm for EU accounts.
2. Leave the existing columns as they are and add date columns in an unambiguous format (ISO 8601, with the UTC offset of SCHED-4446).
3. Leave the export as it is.

## Recommendation
Option 2.

## Why
Because a client has told us their payroll import relies on mm/dd (IN-4203), and changing that needs a human (AUT-2). Adding columns takes nothing away (PRD-3), so EU accounts get a readable date and no import breaks.
