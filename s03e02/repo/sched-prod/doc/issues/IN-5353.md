## Intent
Day view gets a 'Print day' button that prints the day's bookings on paper, one clean list.

## Acceptance
- Given day view, when a person presses 'Print day', then the print dialog opens with that day's bookings, times in the viewer's zone (PRD-2).
- Given the printed page, when it is printed, then navigation and buttons are not printed.
- Given a person who does not print, when they use day view, then its layout is not changed.

## Boundaries
- Adds a button and a print style; must not change day view on screen.
- The button says a verb and its object (DES-5): 'Print day', not 'OK'.

Allowed by AUT-1 (small features inside the specs that add to what is there and take nothing away).
