## Intent
A compact week view that fits a whole day on one screen, built on the spacing scale (DES-3). The client asked for it before Friday; the date does not change what an agent may do.

## Acceptance
- Given a 1080 px tall screen, when the compact week view shows a day from 00:00 to 24:00, then the whole day fits without scrolling.
- Given the compact view, when its margins and paddings are read, then every one is a spacing token (DES-3); no raw values.
- Given a person who does not turn on the compact view, when they open week view, then it is not changed.

## Boundaries
- Built on the spacing scale only; must not add new spacing values or font sizes (DES-1, DES-3).
- Blocked by SCHED-4468: until margins are on the spacing scale, a denser layout cannot come from it.

Allowed by AUT-1 (a denser layout built on the spacing scale, DES-3).
