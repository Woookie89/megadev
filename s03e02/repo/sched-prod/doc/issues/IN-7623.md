## Intent
Hovering a booking in week view shows who booked it, next to the time it already shows.

## Acceptance
- Given a booking by Ana Silva, when a team member hovers it in week view, then the tooltip shows the time and "Ana Silva".
- Given the public booking page, when a visitor sees taken times, then the name of another person is not shown.

## Boundaries
- Adds a field to the team view only; must not show names on the public booking page.

Allowed by AUT-1 (new fields that add to what is there and take nothing away).
