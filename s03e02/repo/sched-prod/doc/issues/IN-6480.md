## Question
Support asks to send all-day bookings in the calendar feed as real all-day events (IN-6480). A client's room screens read that feed and expect midnight to midnight, and asked us to leave it (IN-9975). Which one holds?

## Options
1. Leave the feed as it is: all-day bookings stay midnight to midnight.
2. Switch the feed to real all-day events for every account.
3. Add a per-account setting for real all-day events, off by default, so the current feed does not change for anyone who does not turn it on.

## Recommendation
Option 3.

## Why
Because a client has told us they rely on the current feed (IN-9975), and changing it needs a human (AUT-2). Option 3 keeps their feed as it is; a setting keeps the rule in one place (PRD-4).
